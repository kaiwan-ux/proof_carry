# Claim boundaries

Allowed if supported by results:
- robustness of frozen representations to the registered perturbations,
- safe rejection under insufficient/contradictory evidence,
- differences between hybrid and explicit representations.

Do not claim:
- universal cross-framework migration,
- cryptographic authenticity of receipts,
- tolerance to arbitrary log corruption,
- direct native-checkpoint binary compatibility,
- statistical independence beyond the registered case matrix.
