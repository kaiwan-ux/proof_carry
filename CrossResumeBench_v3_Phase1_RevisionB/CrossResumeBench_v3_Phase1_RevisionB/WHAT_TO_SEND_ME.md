# What to send for analysis

After the run, upload exactly:

`CrossResumeBench_v3_Phase1_RevisionB_results.zip`

Do not delete failed rows or edit CSVs. A failed gate is useful evidence.

The ZIP should include at least:

- `results/self_resume.csv`
- `results/source_capture_results.csv`
- `results/phase1_runs.csv`
- `results/PHASE1_REVISIONB_GATE.json`
- `results/ANALYSIS_SUMMARY.md`
- `results/capabilities.json`
- `results/artifacts/`
- `results/native_sources/`
- `results/native_targets/`
- `results/RESULT_MANIFEST_SHA256.json`
